Executive Outlook

Pune Infrastructure Crisis: A Call for Civic Restoration

Core Sentiment: High frustration characterized by a sense of systemic neglect, particularly regarding basic utility reliability and public safety hazards.

The campaign must pivot from general promises to a "Fix-It-First" platform. By highlighting the high volume of critical, pending infrastructure issues, we can position the incumbent as negligent and ourselves as the party of immediate, tangible action.

Manifesto Foundations
Utility Reliability
Target: Middle-class families and residential housing societies
Promise: "Establish a 24-hour rapid response task force for water and power restoration."
  • No more "pending" status for critical water leaks.
  • Accountability for billing errors and service outages.
  • Modernizing aging pipe and grid infrastructure.
Public Safety and Road Infrastructure
Target: Daily commuters and small business owners
Promise: "Zero-tolerance policy for hazardous road conditions and unfinished construction sites."
  • Immediate repair of sinkholes and bridge cracks.
  • Standardizing road maintenance to prevent accidents.
  • Removing dangerous exposed wires and fallen poles.
